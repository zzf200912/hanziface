Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

HanziFace ("we", "us") operates an online Chinese-language learning service at hanziface.com. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. Contact us at support@hanziface.com with any questions.

1. Information we collect

  • Email address. When you sign in to access premium content, we store the email address you provide. Sign-in uses a passwordless "magic link" — we do not store a password.
  • Learning progress. Your lesson progress, quiz scores and review history are saved in your own browser (local storage) on your device. This data is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Purchase information. Payments are processed by Waffo, our merchant of record. We receive confirmation of your purchase and your contact email so we can enable premium access. We do not collect or store your full card number or bank details.
  • Usage data. We use privacy-friendly, aggregate traffic information (for example, counts of page visits) to understand how the site is used. We do not run advertising trackers or sell your data.

2. How we use your information

  • To deliver the learning service and save your place across visits.
  • To grant and verify access to premium lessons after a purchase.
  • To respond to support requests sent to our contact email.

We do not use your email for unsolicited marketing, and we do not share it with third parties except the service providers strictly needed to operate the site (listed below).

3. Service providers

  • Supabase — hosts our account (magic-link) authentication and stores entitlement records.
  • Waffo — processes payments and acts as merchant of record, handling checkout, tax and receipts under its own privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare — serves the website and protects it from abuse.
Your browser's speech features (text-to-speech and speech recognition used for the speaking practice) run on your device. Audio you speak for practice is processed by your browser's speech service and is not stored by HanziFace.

4. Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. We use browser local storage to keep your learning progress and session state on your device. You can clear this at any time through your browser settings, which will reset your saved progress.

5. Data retention and your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data we hold about you by emailing support@hanziface.com. We will respond within a reasonable period. You can also remove your local learning data at any time by clearing your browser storage.

6. Children's privacy

HanziFace is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service develops. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any change. Material changes will be noted on the site.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, write to support@hanziface.com.
