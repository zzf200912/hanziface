Learn Chinese online — a complete course through shows, games and everyday culture

HanziFace is a structured Chinese course with 50 lessons across nine levels, HSK 1–6 mock exams, AI speaking scores, and spaced-repetition vocabulary. Start with greetings, master daily conversations, and reach confident expression — all through the shows, food, and culture you already love.

L1 — Survival Chinese (12 lessons)

  • Hello! (你好！) — First greetings · survival Chinese
  • Hotpot time! (吃火锅！) — Ordering food · culture: hotpot (火锅)
  • Numbers & age (数字与年龄) — Count 1–100 · asking age
  • At the coffee shop (咖啡店点单) — Ordering drinks · sizes · prices
  • Making new friends (认识新朋友) — Nationalities · talking about studying Chinese
  • Sorry & it's OK (对不起，没关系) — Apologising politely · being forgiven
  • Days of the week (今天星期几？) — Days, today/tomorrow · making plans
  • Meet my family (这是我的家人) — Family members · introducing people
  • Bargaining for fruit (水果市场砍价) — Market culture · 一斤 = 500g · easy bargaining
  • The famous greeting (“你吃了吗？”) — Culture: 吃了吗 is a greeting, not an invitation!
  • Paying by phone (手机支付) — Culture: cash vs WeChat/Alipay QR code
  • Chinese tea culture (中国茶) — Culture: ordering tea · green tea & more

L2 — Daily life (8 lessons)

  • Learn via Chinese shows (追剧学中文) — Culture-IP lesson: Ne Zha / dramas / games phrases
  • Directions & DiDi (问路 & 打车) — Getting around a Chinese city
  • Package delivery (快递到了) — Pickup codes · delivery calls
  • Online shopping (网购达人) — Taobao-style shopping · comparisons · returns
  • Gaming together (一起开黑) — Gamer slang: 开黑 / 辅助 / 输出 / 上线
  • Binge-watching micro-dramas (追微短剧) — Culture IP: China's one-minute drama craze
  • Food delivery (点外卖) — Meituan/Ele.me style ordering · delivery range
  • Giving opinions (我觉得……) — 觉得 / 怎么样 / 不过 · your first opinions in Chinese

L3 — Confident expression (6 lessons)

  • Bargain like a local (砍价高手) — Shopping & bargaining at a market (HSK 3)
  • Job interview (面试自我介绍) — Introduce yourself · why this company · HSK 4-5
  • Meeting a client (见客户谈合作) — Business etiquette · cooperation · details
  • Hotel troubles (酒店有问题) — Complaints politely · upgrades · HSK 5 patterns
  • Chinese internet slang (网络热词) — Culture IP: 内卷 / 躺平 / 热词 — speak like a local
  • Structuring opinions (表达观点) — 一方面……另一方面 · 看法 · HSK 5 connectors
